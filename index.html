About This Lecture Hub

Learn Freelancing AI is a lecture series by Sir Hisham Sarwar on using AI tools such as Claude for freelancing, automation, website development and digital marketing. This Lecture Hub is an independent page made by a student so learners can find each lecture quickly. Every entry shows the lecture number, title and category, and links to the original video on Facebook.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Learn Freelancing AI — Lecture Hub?

It is an independent, student-created directory that collects the links to Sir Hisham Sarwar's Learn Freelancing AI lectures in one place, so learners can find and open them quickly. The videos themselves are on Facebook.

How can I find a specific lecture?

Type a lecture number, such as "Lecture 5", or a topic, such as "SEO", into the search bar. You can also use the category buttons to show only AI & Automation, Freelancing, Web & App Development or Digital Marketing lectures.

Where can I watch the lectures?

On Facebook, where they were originally published. Click "Watch Lecture" to open the original video in a new tab. This site does not host, embed or download any videos.

Do I need Facebook login or group membership?

Some lectures may require you to be logged in to Facebook or to be a member of the Facebook group where the video was shared. Access is controlled by Facebook and the original publisher, not by this site.

What topics are included in the lecture directory?

The lectures cover using Claude AI to automate work, building and publishing websites (including database-driven websites on a hosting server), AI voice agents, Google Search Console and Google Analytics, website SEO audits, and planning and automating social media content with Claude agents.

How can I find Claude AI tutorials?

Type "Claude" in the search bar to see every lecture with Claude in its title, such as "How to Use Claude to Automate Everything".

How often are new lectures added?

New lecture links are added as new lectures are published. The date the list was last updated is shown below the lecture list.

Is this an official Hisham Sarwar website?

No. This is an independent student learning resource. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sir Hisham Sarwar or the course organizers. All lecture content belongs to its original creators.
